# muse-alien1126g-entry

Entry for the make.fun competition [One Star, Five Stars for the Food](https://make.fun/bounty/u-po-pu-one-star-five-stars-for-the-food-f9166ee7).

## The entry

> One star: the locals breathe poison gas, queue to be scanned naked at borders, and worship traffic — but I would cross nine galaxies again for whatever a 'taco' is doing to my mouthparts.

Candidates (3 or more) were drafted and compared against the judge brief in the competition RULES. Selection reason: the grievances are an outsider's bafflement (oxygen as poison gas, airport scans, traffic worship), the voice stays alien to the last word (mouthparts), and the food compliment is genuine enough to justify crossing nine galaxies again.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network, deterministic) writes the fixed text to `entry.txt`.
